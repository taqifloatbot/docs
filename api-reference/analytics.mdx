# Analytics API

The Analytics API provides comprehensive insights into your campaign performance, contact engagement, and marketing automation effectiveness. Track key metrics and optimize your campaigns based on real-time data.

## Overview

Analytics are crucial for understanding campaign effectiveness and optimizing your marketing automation. The Analytics API enables you to:

- **Monitor campaign performance** in real-time
- **Track contact engagement** across all channels
- **Analyze conversion rates** and ROI
- **Identify optimization opportunities**
- **Generate detailed reports** for stakeholders

## Key Metrics

### Campaign Metrics
- **Delivery Rate**: Percentage of messages successfully delivered
- **Open Rate**: Percentage of messages opened (SMS/WhatsApp)
- **Response Rate**: Percentage of contacts who responded
- **Conversion Rate**: Percentage of contacts who completed desired actions
- **Cost per Acquisition**: Average cost to acquire a new customer

### Channel Performance
- **Phone**: Call completion rates, conversation duration, bot interactions
- **SMS**: Delivery rates, open rates, click-through rates
- **WhatsApp**: Message delivery, read receipts, engagement rates

### Contact Engagement
- **Active Contacts**: Contacts who have engaged recently
- **Engagement Score**: Calculated based on interaction history
- **Opt-out Rates**: Percentage of contacts who unsubscribe
- **Re-engagement**: Success rates for win-back campaigns

## Channel-Specific Analytics

### Phone Campaign Analytics
- **Call Completion Rate**: Percentage of calls that connected
- **Average Call Duration**: Mean length of successful calls
- **Bot Interaction Rate**: Percentage of calls that engaged with bot
- **Transfer Rate**: Percentage of calls transferred to human agents
- **Hang-up Rate**: Percentage of calls that ended without completion

### SMS Campaign Analytics
- **Delivery Rate**: SMS messages successfully delivered
- **Open Rate**: Messages opened by recipients
- **Click-through Rate**: Links clicked in SMS messages
- **Response Rate**: SMS replies received
- **Opt-out Rate**: Recipients who unsubscribed

### WhatsApp Campaign Analytics
- **Message Delivery**: WhatsApp messages successfully delivered
- **Read Receipts**: Messages marked as read
- **Response Rate**: WhatsApp replies received
- **Template Performance**: Success rates for different message templates
- **Media Engagement**: Interaction with images, videos, and documents

## Advanced Analytics

### Cohort Analysis
Track contact behavior over time:

- **New Contact Performance**: How new contacts perform in campaigns
- **Retention Rates**: Percentage of contacts who remain engaged
- **Lifetime Value**: Total value generated by contact segments
- **Churn Analysis**: Identify contacts at risk of disengagement

### A/B Testing
Optimize campaigns through testing:

- **Message Variations**: Test different message content
- **Timing Optimization**: Test different send times
- **Channel Performance**: Compare channel effectiveness
- **Audience Segmentation**: Test different contact segments

### Predictive Analytics
Use data to predict future performance:

- **Engagement Prediction**: Identify contacts likely to engage
- **Churn Prediction**: Identify contacts at risk of opting out
- **Optimal Send Times**: Predict best times to contact each segment
- **Content Optimization**: Suggest improvements for better performance

## Integration Options

### Webhook Integration
Receive real-time analytics updates:

```json

  "data": {

    "timestamp": "2024-01-15T10:30:00Z"
```
