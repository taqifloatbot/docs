# Archive Campaign

Archive a campaign to remove it from active management while preserving all data and performance history. Archived campaigns cannot be modified or restarted.

## Overview

The Archive Campaign endpoint allows you to:

- **Remove from Active Management**: Campaigns no longer appear in active campaign lists
- **Preserve Data**: All campaign data, performance metrics, and history are retained
- **Free Resources**: Release system resources used by active campaigns
- **Maintain Compliance**: Keep campaign data for audit and reporting purposes

## When to Archive Campaigns

### Campaign Lifecycle
Campaigns should be archived when they have reached their natural end:

<Steps>
<Step title="Campaign Completion">
  Campaign has finished its scheduled execution period.
</Step>

<Step title="Goal Achievement">
  Campaign has achieved its intended objectives.
</Step>

<Step title="Data Collection">
  Sufficient data has been collected for analysis.
</Step>

<Step title="Archive Decision">
  Decision is made to archive the campaign.
</Step>

<Step title="Archive Execution">
  Campaign is archived and removed from active management.
</Step>
</Steps>

### Archive Criteria
Archive campaigns when they meet these criteria:

- **Completed Successfully**: Campaign finished without errors
- **Reached End Date**: Campaign has passed its scheduled end date
- **Achieved Goals**: Campaign met its performance objectives
- **No Longer Needed**: Campaign is no longer relevant or useful

## Archive vs Delete

### Archive Campaign
- **Data Preserved**: All campaign data and history retained
- **Performance Metrics**: Analytics and reporting data maintained
- **Audit Trail**: Complete audit trail preserved
- **Compliance**: Meets data retention requirements
- **Reversible**: Can be restored if needed (with admin access)

### Delete Campaign
- **Data Lost**: All campaign data permanently removed
- **No Recovery**: Cannot be restored once deleted
- **Compliance Risk**: May violate data retention policies
- **Audit Issues**: Breaks audit trail continuity

<Tip>
**Always Archive**: Use archive instead of delete to maintain data integrity and compliance.
</Tip>

## What Happens When Archived

### Immediate Changes
- **Status Change**: Campaign status changes to "Archived"
- **Removed from Lists**: No longer appears in active campaign lists
- **Stops Execution**: Any ongoing execution is stopped
- **Resource Release**: System resources are freed up

### Data Preservation
- **Campaign Configuration**: All settings and parameters preserved
- **Performance Data**: Complete performance metrics retained
- **Contact Information**: All contact data and interactions maintained
- **Audit Trail**: Complete history of all campaign activities

### Access Changes
- **Read-Only Access**: Can view archived campaigns but cannot modify
- **Reporting Access**: Archived campaigns still appear in reports
- **Analytics Access**: Performance data remains available for analysis
- **Export Access**: Can export archived campaign data

## Error Handling

<AccordionGroup>
<Accordion title="Campaign Not Found">
  - **Error**: `404 Not Found`
  - **Cause**: Campaign ID doesn't exist or user doesn't have access
  - **Solution**: Verify the campaign ID and ensure you have proper permissions
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Campaign Already Archived">
  - **Error**: `400 Bad Request - Campaign already archived`
  - **Cause**: Attempting to archive a campaign that's already archived
  - **Solution**: Check current campaign status before archiving
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Invalid Campaign State">
  - **Error**: `400 Bad Request - Cannot archive campaign in current state`
  - **Cause**: Campaign is in a state that cannot be archived (e.g., actively running)
  - **Solution**: Pause or complete the campaign before archiving
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Access Denied">
  - **Error**: `403 Forbidden`
  - **Cause**: User doesn't have permission to archive campaigns
  - **Solution**: Contact administrator for proper access permissions
</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
